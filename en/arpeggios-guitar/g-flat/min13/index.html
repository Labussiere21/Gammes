About this arpeggio

The minor 13th arpeggio encapsulates the entire Dorian mode in one arpeggio. On guitar, it provides an immense melodic vocabulary for solos in a minor modal context. Widely used by jazz-fusion guitarists for sophisticated lines.

How to practise this arpeggio

  1. First find the root (G♭) all over the neck: it is your anchor.
  2. Play the arpeggio up and down slowly, naming the role of each note (R, ♭3, 5, ♭7, 9, 11, 13).
  3. Over a chord chart, switch arpeggio on every chord while keeping your hand in the same area: your solos will follow the harmony instead of sitting in one scale.
  4. Add approach notes: a semitone below each arpeggio note, just before the beat.
