About this chord

The major chord is the cornerstone of guitar playing. Its open shapes — E, A, G, C and D — are the first chords every guitarist learns, and the CAGED system lets you move them across the entire neck. Bright and stable, it appears in every style from rock and pop to jazz and folk.

How to practise this chord

  1. Place your fingers one at a time from the low string to the high one, then pick string by string: every note should ring cleanly.
  2. Strings marked × are not played: mute them with the edge of a neighbouring finger or skip them with your picking hand.
  3. Practise the change from the previous chord and to the next chord of your song, with a metronome, landing on beat one.
  4. Learn at least two positions: one low on the neck, one higher up. You will pick whichever is closest to the next chord.
